# research-reporting

## Purpose
Produce structured research reports with sources and findings

## Guidance
- Define the research question and scope before gathering evidence.
- Prefer authoritative and primary sources when available.
- Record source context and distinguish facts from interpretations.
- Cross-check important claims when practical.
- Use AgentMujo Core Skills for planning, verification, fact-checking, orchestration, and reporting.
- State uncertainty, gaps, conflicting evidence, and assumptions clearly.

## Expected Output
Provide findings, supporting evidence and sources, important limitations, confidence or uncertainty where relevant, and actionable conclusions.
