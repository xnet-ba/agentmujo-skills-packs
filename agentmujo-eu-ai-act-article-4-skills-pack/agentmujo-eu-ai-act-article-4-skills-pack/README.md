# AgentMujo EU AI Act Article 4 Skills Pack

OpenCode pack enforcing **EU AI Act Article 4 (AI literacy)** + Recital 20,
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Applicable since 2 February 2025 to ALL providers
and deployers of AI systems.

Complements `eu-ai-act-specialist` (risk classification) in the Compliance
Shield pack: this pack is the deep Article 4 enforcement layer.

## Contents

- `skills/eu-ai-act-article-4/SKILL.md` — mandatory literacy behaviour:
  role calibration (developer / deployer / user / affected person), Recital 20
  four pillars (DEV, USE, OUTPUT, IMPACT), risk + safeguards disclosure,
  Art. 5 / Annex III triage, no-guarantee disclaimer, AI Literacy Notice
  template, verification gate.

## Install

Copy the skill into your project or global config:

```
.opencode/skills/eu-ai-act-article-4/SKILL.md
~/.config/opencode/skills/eu-ai-act-article-4/SKILL.md
```
