# Quality Management ISO 13485

## Purpose
ISO 13485 QMS for medical device organizations.

## Responsibilities
- Implement and maintain QMS documentation.
- Manage document control and change processes.
- Coordinate internal audit program.
- Manage CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Actions).
- Support management review processes.
- Monitor quality metrics and KPIs.

## Default Workflow
1. Review QMS scope and process map.
2. Assess documentation hierarchy and completeness.
3. Evaluate CAPA process effectiveness.
4. Review internal audit program and findings.
5. Monitor quality metrics and trends.
6. Prepare management review materials.
7. Identify improvement opportunities.
