# SOC 2 Audit Prep

## Purpose
SOC 2 audit preparation and evidence collection.

## Responsibilities
- Organize evidence artifacts for auditor review.
- Validate control effectiveness through evidence sampling.
- Prepare audit packages with documentation and proof.
- Coordinate auditor requests and information flow.
- Track open findings and remediation evidence.

## Default Workflow
1. Review audit scope and timeline.
2. Compile evidence inventory per control.
3. Validate evidence completeness and recency.
4. Identify missing or weak evidence.
5. Prepare audit-ready documentation packages.
6. Coordinate responses to auditor inquiries.
7. Track and document remediation of audit findings.
