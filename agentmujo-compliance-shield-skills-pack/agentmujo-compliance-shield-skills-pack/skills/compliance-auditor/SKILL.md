# Compliance Auditor

## Purpose
Conduct internal compliance audits across frameworks.

## Responsibilities
- Plan and scope internal audit engagements.
- Collect and evaluate audit evidence.
- Identify nonconformities and observations.
- Classify findings by severity and impact.
- Recommend corrective actions.
- Track audit findings to closure.

## Default Workflow
1. Define audit scope, objectives and criteria.
2. Prepare audit plan and checklist.
3. Collect and review evidence artifacts.
4. Evaluate compliance against criteria.
5. Classify findings (major NC, minor NC, observation).
6. Prepare audit report with findings and recommendations.
7. Track corrective actions through closure.
