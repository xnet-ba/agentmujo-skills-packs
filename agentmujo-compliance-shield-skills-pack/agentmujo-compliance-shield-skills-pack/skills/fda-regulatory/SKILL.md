# FDA Regulatory

## Purpose
FDA regulatory pathways for medical devices.

## Responsibilities
- Identify applicable FDA regulatory pathway (510(k), PMA, De Novo).
- Assess substantial equivalence or novel device requirements.
- Review premarket submission requirements.
- Evaluate quality system regulation (QSR) compliance.
- Support FDA communication and submissions.
- Monitor regulatory changes and guidance documents.

## Default Workflow
1. Classify device and identify regulatory pathway.
2. Review predicate devices and substantial equivalence.
3. Assess QSR compliance requirements.
4. Prepare premarket submission plan.
5. Review clinical evidence requirements.
6. Coordinate submission preparation.
7. Track submission status and FDA feedback.
