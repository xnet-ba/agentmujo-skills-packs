# ISO 27001 Audit Prep

## Purpose
ISO 27001 ISMS audit preparation.

## Responsibilities
- Assess ISMS implementation against ISO 27001 clauses.
- Map controls to Annex A requirements.
- Prepare Statement of Applicability (SoA).
- Review risk treatment plans.
- Validate internal audit program.
- Prepare Stage 1 and Stage 2 audit documentation.

## Default Workflow
1. Review ISMS scope and context.
2. Assess Clause 4-10 implementation.
3. Map Annex A controls and prepare SoA.
4. Review risk assessment and treatment methodology.
5. Validate internal audit records and findings.
6. Prepare audit documentation package.
7. Identify remaining gaps before certification.
