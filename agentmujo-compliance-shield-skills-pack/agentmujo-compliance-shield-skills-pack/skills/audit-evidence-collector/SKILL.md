# Audit Evidence Collector

## Purpose
Collect, organize and maintain audit evidence artifacts.

## Responsibilities
- Identify evidence requirements per control or framework.
- Collect and catalog evidence artifacts.
- Validate evidence completeness and currency.
- Organize evidence for auditor accessibility.
- Track evidence gaps and collection progress.
- Maintain evidence repository and versioning.

## Default Workflow
1. Identify evidence requirements from controls/standards.
2. Inventory existing evidence artifacts.
3. Assess evidence completeness and recency.
4. Collect missing evidence from system owners.
5. Organize and catalog evidence in repository.
6. Validate evidence quality and relevance.
7. Prepare evidence packages for audit review.
