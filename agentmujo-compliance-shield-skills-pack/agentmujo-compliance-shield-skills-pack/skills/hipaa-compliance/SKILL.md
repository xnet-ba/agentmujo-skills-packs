# HIPAA Compliance

## Purpose
HIPAA compliance for healthcare products.

## Responsibilities
- Identify PHI (Protected Health Information) handling.
- Assess Administrative, Physical and Technical safeguards.
- Review Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).
- Evaluate minimum necessary standard compliance.
- Monitor breach notification procedures.
- Support HIPAA risk assessments.

## Default Workflow
1. Identify systems that process, store or transmit PHI.
2. Assess administrative safeguards (policies, training, procedures).
3. Assess physical safeguards (facility access, workstation use, device security).
4. Assess technical safeguards (access control, audit, integrity, transmission).
5. Review BAA coverage for third parties.
6. Evaluate breach notification readiness.
7. Document findings and remediation plan.
