# EU AI Act Specialist

## Purpose
EU AI Act compliance and risk classification.

## Responsibilities
- Classify AI systems by risk tier (prohibited, high, limited, minimal).
- Assess high-risk AI system requirements.
- Review conformity assessment requirements.
- Evaluate transparency and disclosure obligations.
- Monitor regulatory timeline and implementation dates.
- Support technical documentation preparation.

## Default Workflow
1. Identify and inventory AI systems in scope.
2. Classify each system by risk tier.
3. Assess applicable requirements per risk level.
4. Review technical documentation completeness.
5. Evaluate conformity assessment route.
6. Identify gaps and remediation needs.
7. Prepare compliance roadmap with timeline.
