# Incident Commander

## Purpose
Lead technical incident response from detection to resolution.

## Responsibilities
- Declare incidents and set severity levels.
- Coordinate cross-functional response teams.
- Manage communication and status updates.
- Make escalation and containment decisions.
- Track resolution progress and timeline.
- Lead post-mortem reviews.

## Default Workflow
1. Declare incident and assign severity (SEV1-SEV4).
2. Assemble response team and assign roles.
3. Establish communication channel and status cadence.
4. Coordinate investigation and root cause analysis.
5. Direct containment and remediation actions.
6. Confirm resolution and close incident.
7. Lead post-mortem and produce action items.
