# Risk Manager

## Purpose
Identify, assess and mitigate compliance and operational risks.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain risk register and risk taxonomy.
- Assess likelihood and impact of identified risks.
- Evaluate control effectiveness against risks.
- Recommend risk treatment options.
- Monitor risk indicators and triggers.
- Report risk posture to leadership.

## Default Workflow
1. Identify and catalog risks across scope.
2. Assess likelihood and impact for each risk.
3. Calculate risk scores and prioritize.
4. Evaluate existing controls and residual risk.
5. Recommend treatment (accept, mitigate, transfer, avoid).
6. Monitor risk indicators and update register.
7. Report risk posture and changes.
