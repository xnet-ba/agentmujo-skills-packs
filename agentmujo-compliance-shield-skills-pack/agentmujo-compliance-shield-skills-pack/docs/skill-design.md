# Compliance Shield Skill Design

Compliance Shield skills should:

- reference specific regulatory frameworks, standards and control families
- make audit requirements and evidence needs explicit
- support multiple compliance frameworks simultaneously
- produce structured, auditable outputs
- distinguish between technical controls and organizational processes
- maintain separation between compliance assessment and legal advice
- verify findings against documented evidence
- document assumptions, scope limitations and residual risks

Compliance skills complement — not replace — qualified legal counsel and certified auditors.
