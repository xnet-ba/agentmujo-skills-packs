# Product Team Skill Design

Product Team skills should:

- focus on user outcomes, not feature output
- make evidence and assumptions explicit
- support structured prioritization frameworks (RICE, ICE, MoSCoW)
- produce artifacts ready for engineering handoff
- distinguish between discovery and delivery phases
- validate assumptions before committing resources
- maintain user-centricity throughout all workflows
- document decisions and their evidence basis

Product skills complement — not replace — engineering execution and design expertise.
