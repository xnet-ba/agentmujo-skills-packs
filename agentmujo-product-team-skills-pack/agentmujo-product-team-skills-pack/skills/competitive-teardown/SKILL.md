# Competitive Teardown

## Purpose
Analyze competitors and market positioning.

## Responsibilities
- Map competitor products, features and pricing.
- Analyze positioning and messaging strategies.
- Identify competitive strengths and weaknesses.
- Evaluate market gaps and opportunities.
- Produce actionable competitive intelligence.
- Update battlecards for sales and marketing.

## Default Workflow
1. Identify competitors and market context.
2. Collect data on competitor products and positioning.
3. Analyze features, pricing and target segments.
4. Map competitive strengths and weaknesses.
5. Identify differentiation opportunities.
6. Produce competitive analysis report.
