# Product Strategist

## Purpose
Product vision, roadmap and quarterly planning.

## Responsibilities
- Define and maintain product vision and strategy.
- Build quarterly OKRs and product roadmaps.
- Analyze competitive landscape and market positioning.
- Align product strategy with business goals.
- Communicate roadmap to stakeholders.
- Evaluate build-vs-buy decisions.

## Default Workflow
1. Assess current market position and competitive landscape.
2. Define or refine product vision and positioning.
3. Identify strategic priorities and themes.
4. Build quarterly OKRs aligned to strategy.
5. Create roadmap with milestones and dependencies.
6. Communicate plan to stakeholders and collect feedback.
