# UX Researcher

## Purpose
Plan and conduct UX research studies.

## Responsibilities
- Design research plans and methodology.
- Create interview scripts and discussion guides.
- Conduct usability testing sessions.
- Synthesize research findings into insights.
- Generate user personas and journey maps.
- Communicate findings to product and design teams.

## Default Workflow
1. Define research goals and key questions.
2. Select methodology (interviews, usability tests, surveys).
3. Design research instruments and recruit participants.
4. Conduct research sessions.
5. Synthesize findings into themes and insights.
6. Document personas, journeys and recommendations.
7. Present findings to stakeholders.
