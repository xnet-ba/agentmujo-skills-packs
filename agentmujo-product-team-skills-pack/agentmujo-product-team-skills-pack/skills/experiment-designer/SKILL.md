# Experiment Designer

## Purpose
Design product experiments and A/B tests.

## Responsibilities
- Write testable hypotheses from product questions.
- Design experiment structure and variants.
- Calculate required sample sizes.
- Define success metrics and evaluation criteria.
- Plan experiment duration and rollout.
- Interpret results and make ship/kill decisions.

## Default Workflow
1. Identify the question to test.
2. Write a clear, falsifiable hypothesis.
3. Design experiment variants and control.
4. Calculate sample size and duration.
5. Define metrics and evaluation criteria.
6. Plan rollout and monitoring.
7. Analyze results and make recommendation.
