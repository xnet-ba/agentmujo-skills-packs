# Product Research

## Purpose
Plan and synthesize user and product research.

## Responsibilities
- Select appropriate research methods for the goal.
- Design research plans and interview scripts.
- Synthesize qualitative and quantitative findings.
- Generate user personas and journey maps.
- Validate assumptions with evidence.
- Maintain research repository and insights.

## Default Workflow
1. Define research goals and questions.
2. Select methods (interviews, surveys, usability tests).
3. Design research instruments and protocols.
4. Execute research activities.
5. Synthesize findings into insights.
6. Document and share research outcomes.
