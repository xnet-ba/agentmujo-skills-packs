# Launch Strategy

## Purpose
Plan and execute product launches.

## Responsibilities
- Design launch plans with phases and milestones.
- Coordinate cross-functional launch activities.
- Define launch metrics and success criteria.
- Plan go-to-market alignment with marketing.
- Manage launch checklists and readiness gates.
- Coordinate post-launch monitoring and iteration.

## Default Workflow
1. Define launch objectives and success metrics.
2. Identify launch type (soft, beta, GA).
3. Create launch plan with timeline and owners.
4. Coordinate marketing, sales and support readiness.
5. Define launch gates and readiness criteria.
6. Execute launch plan.
7. Monitor post-launch metrics and iterate.
