# Roadmap Planner

## Purpose
Build and communicate product roadmaps.

## Responsibilities
- Structure roadmaps by themes, time horizons or outcomes.
- Align roadmap items with product strategy.
- Manage dependencies and sequencing.
- Communicate roadmap to different audiences.
- Track progress and update timelines.
- Balance now/next/later priorities.

## Default Workflow
1. Review product strategy and quarterly OKRs.
2. Gather input from stakeholders and research.
3. Structure roadmap by themes or time horizons.
4. Sequence items by dependencies and priority.
5. Communicate roadmap to relevant audiences.
6. Track progress and update as needed.
