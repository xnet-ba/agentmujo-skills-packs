# Product Discovery

## Purpose
Validate opportunities and test problem-solution fit.

## Responsibilities
- Identify and validate product opportunities.
- Test problem-solution fit before building.
- Design discovery sprints and experiments.
- Synthesize discovery findings into actionable insights.
- Determine readiness for delivery phase.
- Kill or pivot invalid ideas early.

## Default Workflow
1. Identify the opportunity or hypothesis.
2. Design a discovery approach (interviews, prototypes, tests).
3. Execute discovery activities.
4. Synthesize findings and validate assumptions.
5. Assess problem-solution fit.
6. Decide: proceed, pivot, or kill.
