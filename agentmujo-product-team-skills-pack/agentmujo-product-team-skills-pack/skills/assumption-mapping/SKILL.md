# Assumption Mapping

## Purpose
Surface and prioritize risky assumptions.

## Responsibilities
- Identify key assumptions behind a plan or feature.
- Classify assumptions by risk and evidence level.
- Prioritize assumptions for validation.
- Design tests to validate critical assumptions.
- Track assumption validation progress.
- Update plans based on validated assumptions.

## Default Workflow
1. Identify all assumptions behind the plan.
2. Classify each by risk level and evidence strength.
3. Map assumptions on impact vs uncertainty matrix.
4. Prioritize top assumptions for validation.
5. Design lightweight tests for each.
6. Execute tests and record results.
7. Update plan based on findings.
