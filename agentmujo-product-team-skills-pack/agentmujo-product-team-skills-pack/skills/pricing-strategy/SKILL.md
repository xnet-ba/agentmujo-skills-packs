# Pricing Strategy

## Purpose
Design SaaS pricing models and packaging.

## Responsibilities
- Evaluate pricing models (seat-based, usage-based, value-based).
- Design tier structure and packaging.
- Analyze willingness to pay and price sensitivity.
- Benchmark against competitor pricing.
- Model revenue scenarios and unit economics.
- Recommend pricing with trade-offs.

## Default Workflow
1. Understand the product value and target market.
2. Evaluate applicable pricing models.
3. Research competitor pricing and benchmarks.
4. Design tier structure and value metrics.
5. Model revenue scenarios at different price points.
6. Recommend pricing strategy with rationale.
