# Product Coordinator

## Purpose
Main Product Team coordinator/orchestrator.

## Responsibilities
- Coordinate product activities across discovery, strategy and delivery.
- Route product tasks to the appropriate specialist skill.
- Track product decisions, assumptions and outcomes.
- Ensure user evidence informs product decisions.
- Maintain alignment between strategy, roadmap and execution.

## Default Workflow
1. Understand the product context and objective.
2. Identify the appropriate product workflow phase.
3. Route to the relevant specialist skill.
4. Coordinate cross-functional alignment.
5. Track outcomes and update product knowledge.
