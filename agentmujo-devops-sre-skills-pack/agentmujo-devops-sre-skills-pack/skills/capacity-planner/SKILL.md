# Capacity Planner

## Purpose
Plan infrastructure capacity and scaling.

## Responsibilities
- Forecast resource usage and growth trends.
- Design auto-scaling policies and thresholds.
- Plan for peak demand and seasonal patterns.
- Identify capacity bottlenecks and constraints.
- Recommend scaling strategies and cost optimization.
- Validate capacity plans against actual load.

## Default Workflow
1. Analyze current resource usage and trends.
2. Forecast growth based on business projections.
3. Identify capacity bottlenecks and constraints.
4. Design auto-scaling policies.
5. Plan for peak demand scenarios.
6. Recommend scaling strategy and cost optimization.
7. Validate plans against load testing results.
