# Email Sequence

## Purpose
Design email drip campaigns and nurture sequences.

## Responsibilities
- Map email sequences to buyer journey stages.
- Write subject lines and email copy.
- Design timing, triggers and segmentation logic.
- Optimize open rates, click rates and conversions.
- A/B test subject lines and content variants.
- Track sequence performance and iterate.

## Default Workflow
1. Define sequence goal and audience segment.
2. Map emails to journey stages and triggers.
3. Write subject lines and body copy for each email.
4. Design timing and segmentation logic.
5. Set up tracking and measurement.
6. Monitor performance and optimize.
