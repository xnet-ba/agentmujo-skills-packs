# Cold Email

## Purpose
Write and optimize B2B cold outreach emails.

## Responsibilities
- Research prospects and personalize outreach.
- Write compelling subject lines and opening lines.
- Apply proven cold email frameworks.
- Design follow-up sequences.
- Optimize reply rates and meeting bookings.
- Test and iterate on messaging variants.

## Default Workflow
1. Research the prospect and their context.
2. Identify the relevant pain point or trigger.
3. Write personalized subject and opening line.
4. Craft the core value proposition.
5. Design CTA and follow-up sequence.
6. Test and optimize response rates.
