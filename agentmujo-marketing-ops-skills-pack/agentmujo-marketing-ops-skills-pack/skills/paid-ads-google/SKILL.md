# Paid Ads Google

## Purpose
Create and optimize Google Ads campaigns.

## Responsibilities
- Design campaign structure and ad groups.
- Write responsive search ads and extensions.
- Research and select keywords with intent mapping.
- Set bid strategies and budget allocation.
- Optimize Quality Score and ad relevance.
- Analyze campaign performance and ROI.

## Default Workflow
1. Define campaign objective and target audience.
2. Research keywords and search intent.
3. Design campaign structure and ad groups.
4. Write ad copy and extensions.
5. Set bidding strategy and budget.
6. Launch and monitor performance.
7. Optimize based on data.
