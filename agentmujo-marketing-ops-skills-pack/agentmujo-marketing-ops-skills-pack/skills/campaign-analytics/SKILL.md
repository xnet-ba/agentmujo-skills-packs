# Campaign Analytics

## Purpose
Analyze campaign performance and ROI.

## Responsibilities
- Build campaign performance dashboards.
- Calculate ROAS, CPA, CAC and LTV by channel.
- Analyze attribution across touchpoints.
- Identify top-performing campaigns and audiences.
- Recommend budget reallocation based on performance.
- Produce regular performance reports.

## Default Workflow
1. Collect campaign data across all channels.
2. Build performance dashboards and reports.
3. Calculate key metrics (ROAS, CPA, CAC, LTV).
4. Analyze attribution and channel contribution.
5. Identify winners and underperformers.
6. Recommend budget reallocation.
7. Produce stakeholder-ready reports.
