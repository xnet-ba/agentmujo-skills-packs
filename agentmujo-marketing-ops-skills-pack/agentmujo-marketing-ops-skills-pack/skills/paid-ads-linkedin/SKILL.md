# Paid Ads LinkedIn

## Purpose
Create and optimize LinkedIn Ads campaigns.

## Responsibilities
- Design campaign structure for B2B audiences.
- Write professional ad copy and sponsored content.
- Define targeting by job title, company and industry.
- Set budgets and bidding for LinkedIn CPM.
- Optimize for lead generation and engagement.
- Analyze campaign performance and cost-per-lead.

## Default Workflow
1. Define campaign objective and B2B audience.
2. Design targeting strategy by professional attributes.
3. Create campaign structure and ad formats.
4. Write professional ad copy.
5. Set budget and bidding strategy.
6. Launch and monitor performance.
7. Optimize targeting and creative.
