# Marketing Ops Skill Design

Marketing Ops skills should:

- focus on measurable conversion improvements and ROI
- produce A/B testable variants and hypotheses
- ground recommendations in analytics data and user behavior
- apply proven copywriting frameworks (AIDA, PAS, BAB)
- distinguish between traffic acquisition and conversion optimization
- make assumptions and test results explicit
- optimize for the full funnel, not isolated metrics
- verify claims against actual performance data

Marketing Ops skills complement — not replace — brand strategy and creative direction.
